Orlando is not a beach town, and that changes what an e-bike is for here. Nobody is cruising a
seafront promenade. What Orlando has instead is one of the best rail trails in the state twenty
minutes from the resort corridor, a genuinely charming small town sitting on it, and the only rolling
terrain in Florida a short drive further west.

This list covers shops within about 25 miles of Orlando - the wider radius reflects how spread out
Central Florida is - ranked by Google star rating weighted against review volume.

## Where to actually ride

**The West Orange Trail, Winter Garden.** The reason to rent in Orlando. Twenty-two miles of paved
rail trail running through Oakland and Winter Garden, with a brick main street, a farmers market and
a row of restaurants sitting directly on the path. The Oakland to Winter Garden stretch is about 8
miles round trip with genuine oak shade. Full detail in our
[West Orange Trail guide](/trails/west-orange-trail/).

**Clermont and the Lake Minneola Scenic Trail.** Half an hour west, and the only place in Florida
with hills worth the name. Florida's cycling teams train here. On a pedal bike it is a serious ride;
on an e-bike it is an afternoon with views you cannot get anywhere else in the state.

**Shingle Creek Trail.** Closer to the resort corridor, running through wetland and cypress. Useful
if you do not want to drive.

**Lake Nona and Baldwin Park.** Suburban, well surfaced, quiet. Good for an easy family loop.

**Not International Drive.** Visitors ask. The answer is no: heavy traffic, constant driveways, and
nothing you want to see at bike speed.

## Renting around the theme parks

If you are staying in the resort corridor, two things are worth knowing.

**Most rental shops are not near you.** Orlando's e-bike shops cluster in Winter Garden, Clermont
and the residential neighbourhoods rather than along the resort strip. Factor in a drive, or ask
about delivery.

**A trail day is the best day off you can take.** Winter Garden is roughly twenty minutes from the
main resort areas, and the contrast - a quiet brick main street and a shaded rail trail - is exactly
what a theme park week needs on day four.

Some shops will deliver to hotels and vacation rentals in the resort corridor. It is worth asking
when you call, and worth paying for if there are three or more of you. Our
[delivery cost guide](/costs/ebike-delivery-fees-florida/) covers typical fees.

## What to rent

**Class 1 or Class 2 hybrids and cruisers** for the rail trails. The riding is flat, paved and
uninterrupted, which is the easiest riding in the state.

**More assist for Clermont.** If you are heading to the hills, ask specifically about motor torque
and battery capacity rather than just the class. A bike that is fine on the West Orange Trail will
drain fast on repeated climbs.

**Family gear.** Trailers and tag-alongs work particularly well on rail trails, where the surface is
smooth and there is room to pass.

## Heat, timing and season

Orlando is inland, which means hotter than the coast with no sea breeze. From May to September, ride
before 10am. The Oakland stretch of the West Orange Trail has real canopy, which makes it one of the
more survivable summer rides in Central Florida.

Peak season is spring break, summer and the December holidays - the theme park calendar rather than
the beach one. Winter Garden gets busy at weekends year-round, especially on farmers market days.

## Rules worth knowing

Florida treats an e-bike with a 750 W or smaller motor and working pedals as a bicycle: no licence,
registration or insurance. Helmets are required under 16, and Class 3 bikes carry a minimum
operating age of 16.

Shared-use paths are where the local rules bite. Trail systems can restrict faster Class 3 bikes
while permitting Class 1 and 2, and Winter Garden applies dismount zones through the busiest
downtown blocks at weekends. Ask your rental shop, and read the signs at the trailhead.

## FAQs

### Where is the best place to rent an e-bike near Orlando?

Winter Garden, for most visitors, because it puts you directly on the West Orange Trail. Clermont is
the alternative if you want the hills. The shops at the top of the list above have the strongest
rating and review combination within 25 miles of Orlando.

### Can I rent an e-bike near the theme parks?

Some shops deliver to hotels and vacation rentals in the resort corridor, but most Orlando e-bike
shops are in Winter Garden, Clermont and the residential neighbourhoods rather than on the strip.
Ask about delivery when you call, or plan a twenty-minute drive to Winter Garden.

### Is there anywhere good to ride an e-bike in Orlando?

Yes - the West Orange Trail is one of the best rides in Florida, and the Lake Minneola Scenic Trail
around Clermont has the only real hills in the state. Shingle Creek Trail is the closest option to
the resort corridor.

### How much does it cost to rent an e-bike in Orlando?

Roughly $30 to $55 for two hours, $60 to $95 for a full day, and $200 to $375 for a week. Inland
Central Florida generally runs a little cheaper than the beach towns, with a card hold of $100 to
$300 per bike.

### Is the West Orange Trail suitable for children?

It is one of the best family rides in Florida: flat, paved, separated from traffic, well signposted,
and it ends in a town with ice cream. The Oakland stretch has the most shade and the fewest road
crossings.

### Is riding in Orlando worth it if I am here for the parks?

A half day on the West Orange Trail is the best break from the parks available, and it is about
twenty minutes from the main resort areas. Ride in the morning, eat on Plant Street in Winter
Garden, and be back for an afternoon at the hotel pool.

## The short version

Orlando riding means driving twenty minutes to Winter Garden and getting on the West Orange Trail,
or an extra fifteen to Clermont if you want hills. Rent a Class 1 or 2 for the rail trail, ask for
more assist if you are heading to the climbs, and go early in summer.

Then call the shop before you drive - inland Central Florida shops keep more conventional hours than
the beach towns, and several are closed Sundays.
