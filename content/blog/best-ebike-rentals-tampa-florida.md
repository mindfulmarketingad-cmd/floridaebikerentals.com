Tampa is the Tampa Bay city people forget to ride in. The beaches and the Pinellas Trail are across
the bay in Pinellas County, so most visitors assume the bikes are over there too. But Tampa has two
of the best short urban rides in Florida - the Riverwalk and Bayshore Boulevard - joined end to end,
flat, on the water and almost entirely off the road. On an e-bike you can ride both, stop for lunch,
and still be back before the afternoon storms roll in.

This list covers shops within about 15 miles of downtown Tampa, ranked by Google star rating weighted
against review volume. The tighter radius keeps it to the Tampa side of the bay; for St. Petersburg,
Dunedin and Clearwater, see our [Pinellas Trail guide](/trails/pinellas-trail/).

## Where the shops are, and why it matters

Tampa's rental shops are spread out, and where you pick up decides what you ride.

**South Tampa, for Bayshore.** Several shops sit on or near South MacDill Avenue and Dale Mabry
Highway, a short ride from Bayshore Boulevard. Tampa Bay eBikes' Tampa showroom on South MacDill
rents e-bikes, runs guided rides and lists delivery; Outspokin Bicycles is on South Dale Mabry.

**Downtown, for the Riverwalk.** City Bike Tampa on East Cass Street is a few blocks from the
Riverwalk and lists delivery. Tampa Riverwalk Rentals launches from the Marriott Water Street, right
on the path - but its rental fleet is pedal Coastal Cruisers, not e-bikes.

**North Tampa and the suburbs.** UBC - University Bicycle Center is on Fletcher Avenue near USF, and
also runs a rental shop inside Alafia River State Park in Lithia. Citrus Park Bikes, in the
northwest, sits close to the Upper Tampa Bay Trail. Pedego Electric Bikes Tampa is on North Himes
Avenue.

Several Tampa shops list delivery. If you are staying in a downtown hotel or a South Tampa rental and
there are two or more of you, ask - our [delivery fee guide](/costs/ebike-delivery-fees-florida/)
covers what that usually adds.

## Where to actually ride

**The Tampa Riverwalk (2.6 miles).** A multi-use path along the Hillsborough River and the Garrison
Channel, from Armature Works and the Heights at the north end, past Curtis Hixon Park and the
museums, to Sparkman Wharf. It is the city's showpiece, which is also its problem: it is busy with
walkers, especially on weekend evenings. Treat it as a slow cruise, not a ride to cover miles.

**Bayshore Boulevard (4.5 miles).** A 10-foot-wide waterfront sidewalk from Platt Street to Gandy
Boulevard, along Hillsborough Bay, past the historic houses of Hyde Park. It is one of the longest
continuous sidewalks in the United States, and the classic Tampa ride. There are on-road bike lanes
in both directions along most of the boulevard too, which is where faster riders belong. Out and back
is about 9 miles; Hyde Park Village is the obvious stop.

**Riverwalk plus Bayshore.** The two meet at the Platt Street Bridge on the south side of downtown,
which makes a natural half-day:
start at Armature Works, ride the Riverwalk south, cross onto Bayshore, ride to Gandy and back. With
a lunch stop it fills a two- to four-hour rental comfortably.

**The Selmon Greenway (1.7 miles).** An east-west path through downtown, mostly in the shade of the
expressway, connecting with the Riverwalk and the Meridian Avenue Trail toward the Channel District.
Useful on a hot afternoon.

**Davis Islands.** Just across the channel from downtown and quiet by Tampa standards: palm-lined
streets, the waterfront and the small airport. Local clubs ride laps of the island; on a rental it
makes a pleasant add-on to Bayshore.

**The Upper Tampa Bay Trail (7.25 miles).** A paved, separated trail in northwest Hillsborough
County, with bridges and underpasses, and a better choice than the city if you want distance without
pedestrians. Citrus Park Bikes is the shop on this side of town.

**Alafia River State Park.** For off-road riding, the park southeast of the city is known for its
mountain bike trails, and UBC has a rental shop inside it. Ask for a bike built for dirt - a city
cruiser is the wrong tool.

## The rules on Tampa's trails in 2026

Florida treats an e-bike with a 750 W or smaller motor and working pedals as a bicycle: no licence,
registration or insurance, and a helmet is required under 16. The full picture is in our
[Florida e-bike laws guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/). Tampa adds a few things on top.

- **E-bikes are allowed on city trails.** The City of Tampa's greenways and trails FAQ lists electric
  bicycles as permitted on city trails, with a 15 mph speed limit on all of them. Gas-powered bikes
  are not allowed.
- **The Riverwalk is slower in practice.** It is a crowded shared path, and the guideline on the
  Riverwalk has been 5 mph. Tampa City Council has already voted down an ordinance that would have
  set a 10 mph limit on city trails and banned wheelies and riding more than two abreast, and sent it
  back to public workshops. Expect the issue to return.
- **Hillsborough County is moving now.** A county ordinance proposed at the Sheriff's Office's
  request would cap e-bikes, scooters and bicycles at 10 mph on sidewalks, 15 mph on shared paths and
  trails and 28 mph on roads and bike lanes, ban wheelies and stunts, and let deputies cite riders
  and impound bikes. As reported in October 2026, it goes to a public hearing in November, and it
  would apply in Tampa wherever the city has not set its own rules.

None of that changes what a sensible visitor does anyway: ride slowly where people are walking, ring
or call out before passing, and use the road bike lanes on Bayshore if you want to go faster. Rules
are moving quickly in Tampa Bay, so ask your rental shop what currently applies on the day you ride.

## What to rent

**A Class 1 or Class 2 cruiser or step-through.** Tampa's rides are flat, paved and short. Comfort
and an upright position matter more than range or speed, and Class 1 and 2 bikes stop assisting at
20 mph anyway.

**Something lighter for downtown.** If you plan to hop between the Riverwalk, Ybor and Sparkman
Wharf, a lighter bike is easier to lift onto racks and wheel through crowds. Our
[lightweight e-bikes guide](/electric-bike-types/lightweight-electric-bikes/) explains the trade-off.

**A real mountain bike for Alafia.** Do not take a rental cruiser onto singletrack.

Check what is included before you go. Helmets and locks are commonly included in Tampa rentals but not
universally, and shops ask for a card hold - see our
[deposits and card holds guide](/costs/ebike-rental-deposits-and-card-holds/) and the
[pre-rental checklist](/blog/ebike-rental-checklist/).

## What it costs

Prices vary by shop, so check each one. As examples from shops' own sites in October 2026: Tampa

## When to ride

**November to April is the season.** Cooler, drier and the best time of year to ride in Tampa. It is
also when the Riverwalk is busiest, especially on weekend evenings and during downtown events, so go
in the morning if you want room.

**Summer means early starts.** From June to September, ride before 10am and expect thunderstorms most
afternoons. The Selmon Greenway's expressway shade and the breeze off the bay on Bayshore help, but
there is little tree cover on either the Riverwalk or Bayshore.

**Watch the event calendar.** Big downtown days - Gasparilla in January above all - close streets
and pack the waterfront. Book ahead, or plan to ride somewhere else that day.

## FAQs

### Where is the best place to rent an e-bike in Tampa?

South Tampa or downtown, for most visitors, because that puts you minutes from Bayshore Boulevard
and the Riverwalk. The shops at the top of the list above have the strongest rating and review
combination within 15 miles of downtown. Check each listing's services first: some Tampa shops rent
only pedal bikes.

### Can you ride an e-bike on the Tampa Riverwalk?

Yes. The City of Tampa allows electric bicycles on its trails, including the Riverwalk, which was
built as a multi-use path. The city trail speed limit is 15 mph, but the Riverwalk is crowded with
walkers and its guideline has been much slower, so ride it at walking-plus pace.

### Can you ride an e-bike on Bayshore Boulevard?

Yes. You can ride the wide waterfront sidewalk, shared with walkers and runners, or the on-road bike
lanes. Faster riders should use the bike lanes. Hillsborough County's proposed ordinance would cap
sidewalk speeds at 10 mph, so expect slower limits on the sidewalk.

### How long does it take to ride Bayshore Boulevard?

The sidewalk is 4.5 miles from Platt Street to Gandy Boulevard, so out and back is about 9 miles.
At an easy e-bike pace that is under an hour of riding, which is why most people add the Riverwalk,
Davis Islands or a long lunch in Hyde Park.

### Are there e-bike speed limits in Tampa?

City trails have a 15 mph limit, and a slower guideline applies on the Riverwalk. Hillsborough
County is considering countywide limits of 10 mph on sidewalks, 15 mph on shared paths and 28 mph on
roads and bike lanes. Ask your rental shop what is in force when you ride.

### Is Tampa or St. Petersburg better for an e-bike day?

Tampa for a short, scenic city ride on the Riverwalk and Bayshore. St. Petersburg if you want a
long day: the Pinellas Trail runs 45 miles north to Tarpon Springs. Our
[Pinellas Trail guide](/trails/pinellas-trail/) covers the sections worth riding.

## The short version

Rent in South Tampa or downtown, ride the Riverwalk and Bayshore Boulevard as one half-day loop, and
add Davis Islands or Ybor if you have time. Go slowly where people are walking, use the Bayshore bike
lanes if you want to go faster, and keep an eye on Hillsborough County's new e-bike rules. Want
distance instead? Head northwest to the Upper Tampa Bay Trail, or across the bay to the Pinellas
Trail.
