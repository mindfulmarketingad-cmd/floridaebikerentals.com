## Safety

Electric bikes are faster and heavier than conventional bicycles. Riding involves risk of injury or
death. Nothing on this site should be treated as safety instruction or as confirmation that any
operator meets any standard of care.

Before you ride: wear a helmet, check local rules for where e-bikes are permitted, follow the
operator's instructions, ride within your ability, and never let a child ride equipment rated for
adults. Florida law and local ordinances on e-bike class, sidewalk riding, beach access and minimum
age change, and they vary between cities and counties. Verify current rules with the operator and
the local authority.
