## The short version

Florida Ebike Rentals is a static directory. We do not require an account, we do not sell personal
information, and we collect as little as the site can function on. The two places data does get
involved are advertising, which is served by Google, and the contact form, which you choose to
send us.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** If you use the [contact form](/contact/), we receive whatever you put
in it: your name, email address, and the content of your message. We use it to answer you and to
correct the directory. We do not add you to a marketing list.

**Automatically collected technical data.** Like nearly every website, our hosting provider records
standard server data when a page is requested — IP address, browser user agent, referring page,
and the time of the request. This is used for security, abuse prevention and aggregate traffic
counts.

**Location, only if you allow it.** The homepage can show rentals near you. That feature calls your
browser's geolocation API, which always asks your permission first. Your coordinates are used inside
your own browser to sort the list of listings and are never transmitted to us or stored anywhere.
Decline the prompt and the site works normally, showing top-rated statewide results instead.

**What we do not collect.** No accounts, no passwords, no payment details. We never take a booking,
so we never handle payment card data.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set our own tracking or analytics cookies. The site's own functionality runs without
them.

Third parties on this site may set cookies or use similar identifiers:

- **Google AdSense** serves the advertising that funds this site. Google and its partners may use
  cookies and device identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your
  prior visits to this and other sites.
- **Map tiles** are loaded from OpenStreetMap when you open a map view. Your browser requests those
  images directly from OpenStreetMap's servers, which receive your IP address as part of any normal
  web request.
- **Listing photographs** are loaded from Google's image servers for the same reason.

## Advertising and Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to
this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to
serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting
[Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can opt out of some third-party
vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at
[aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or, in Europe,
[Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).

For more information about how Google handles data on partner sites, see
[Google's policy page](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## How we use information

We use the limited information described above to:

- respond to messages you send us,
- correct, add or remove directory listings,
- keep the site available and defend against abuse,
- understand aggregate traffic patterns,
- serve and measure advertising through Google AdSense.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share your contact-form message with the
businesses listed on this site unless you specifically ask us to pass it on.

## Data retention

Contact-form messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and to maintain a
record of listing corrections, then deleted. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider on
their standard schedule, typically a short rolling window.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information
we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to certain processing, or to opt out
of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as those terms are defined by state privacy laws
such as the Florida Digital Bill of Rights, the California Consumer Privacy Act and the GDPR.

We do not sell personal information. To exercise any other right, contact us through the
[contact page](/contact/) and tell us what you would like us to do. We will respond within the
time frame your local law requires.

## Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal
information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. It is a static site with no user accounts, no database of visitors
and no login system, which removes the most common ways personal data leaks from a website. No
method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we hold almost nothing that
would be worth taking.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The revision date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
Material changes will be reflected here rather than sent by email, since we do not hold a mailing
list.

## Contact

Privacy questions go through our [contact page](/contact/).
