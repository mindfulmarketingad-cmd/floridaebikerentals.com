## How can we help?

Use the form below for anything to do with the directory. We answer listing corrections first,
usually within two business days.

{{CONTACT_FORM}}

## What to send us

**Correcting a listing.** Include the listing URL and the field that is wrong (hours, phone,
address, website, or the business has closed). We verify against the business's own site or a
public source before changing anything.

**Adding your shop.** Send the business name, full street address, phone number, website and the
services you offer — e-bike rental, delivery, guided tours, repairs. Listings are free. We only
publish businesses that rent or sell electric bikes and bicycles somewhere in Florida.

**Removing a listing.** If you own or manage a business on this site and want it removed, say so
and we will take the page down. You do not have to give a reason.

**Advertising and partnerships.** Tell us the campaign, the regions you care about and rough
timing. We will reply with what is available.

**Anything else.** Press, data questions, corrections to our guides — same form.

## What we cannot do

We are an independent directory, not a rental company. If you booked through us with Book Through Us,
contact us here to change or cancel it ([our cancellation terms](/terms/#book-through-us)). If you
booked directly with a shop, changes, refunds and deposits go to that shop. For a new rental, use our
[e-bike finder](/#finder): every listing has the shop's phone number, website and directions.

## Response times

Most messages get an answer within two business days. Listing corrections are prioritised.
During peak Florida travel weeks, expect a little longer.
