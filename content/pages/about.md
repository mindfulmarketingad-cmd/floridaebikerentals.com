## Why we built Florida Ebike Rentals

Renting an e-bike should be the easiest part of a Florida holiday. In practice it is one of the
most frustrating. Search for a rental in a town you have never visited and you get a mix of
national booking platforms, expired listings, shops that closed two seasons ago, and results for a
city three hours away. Families lose an afternoon of their trip to the search instead of the ride.

Florida Ebike Rentals fixes one narrow problem well: showing you every operating e-bike rental
shop, bike rental service and guided tour operator in a Florida town, with the practical details you
need before you pick up the phone. Hours. Address. Phone number. Website. What people actually
rate the shop on Google. Whether they run tours or only hand over keys at a counter.

## What is on the site

- **[Partner listings](/partners/)** — a page for every rental shop and e-bike store we track,
  built from public Google Business data and refreshed periodically.
- **[Find pages](/find/)** — city and region guides that answer "where do I rent an e-bike in
  Key West / Destin / Naples", ranked so the best-reviewed shops sit at the top.
- **[Reviews](/reviews/)** — the rating breakdown behind each shop's star average, so you can see
  whether a 4.6 comes from 40 reviews or 4,000.
- **[Blog guides](/blog/)** — riding routes, Florida e-bike law, pricing, safety with kids, and
  the practical questions that come up before a first rental.
- **[Search](/search/)** — one box across every page on the site.

## How we choose what to list

Every listing on this site comes from public business data: name, address, phone number, website,
opening hours, category, and the Google review count and star average. We import that data, then
apply three filters before a business gets a page.

1. **It must be in Florida.** Nothing outside the state is published, even when it shows up in a
   regional search.
2. **It must be open.** Businesses marked permanently or temporarily closed are removed at import,
   which is why the directory is smaller than a raw scrape of "bike rental Florida" would be.
3. **It must actually serve riders.** A shop qualifies when it rents bikes, sells or services
   electric bikes, or runs guided rides. Phone repair counters and clothing stores that happen to
   carry one e-bike brand do not make the cut.

We do not rank shops by who pays us. There is no paid placement on this site. Ordering on our
listicles is driven by a blend of Google star rating and review volume, so a shop with 4.9 stars
across 800 reviews outranks a 5.0 with three reviews — that is deliberate, because a handful of
reviews is not yet evidence.

## What we are not

We are a directory, not a rental company and not a booking agent. We do not take payments, hold
reservations, set prices, deliver bikes, or handle refunds. When you rent, your agreement is with
the shop, not with us. Confirm price, deposit, helmet policy, minimum age and cancellation terms
directly with the business before you pay anything.

We also do not write the reviews. Star ratings and review counts shown on this site are Google's,
reproduced as data points and refreshed on a schedule. They can lag behind reality, so treat them as
a starting signal rather than a guarantee.

## Keeping the data honest

Business data changes constantly. Shops move, change hours, get bought, or shut down between our
imports. Two things help us keep up:

- **Scheduled re-imports.** The whole directory is rebuilt from a fresh data pull, which removes
  closed businesses and picks up new ones.
- **You.** If a listing is wrong, out of date, or should not be here at all, tell us on the
  [contact page](/contact/) and we will correct or remove it. Shop owners can request edits to
  their own listing the same way, at no charge.

## How the site pays for itself

Florida Ebike Rentals is free to use and free to be listed in. The site is supported by
advertising, which is clearly marked wherever it appears. Advertising has no influence over which
businesses are listed or how they are ordered. Read the full detail in our
[disclaimer](/disclaimer/) and [privacy policy](/privacy/).
