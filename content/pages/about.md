## We make finding an e-bike in Florida easy

Florida Ebike Rentals exists for one reason: to make finding e-bike rentals and e-bike services in
Florida easy. Whether you need a beach cruiser for a week on 30A, a guided ride in Key West, a shop
that delivers bikes to your rental house, or someone to look at a tired battery, you should be able
to find it in a minute, not lose an afternoon of your trip to searching.

Search for a rental in a town you have never visited and you usually get a mix of booking platforms,
listings for shops that have closed, and results for a city three hours away. We built this site to
cut through that: every operating rental shop and e-bike service we can find, town by town, with
what you need to choose one.

## How we choose and rank shops

Every listing starts from public business data: name, address, opening hours, category, services,
and the Google review count and star average. We import that data, then apply three filters before
a business appears:

1. **It must be in Florida.** Nothing outside the state is published.
2. **It must be open.** Businesses marked permanently or temporarily closed are removed at import.
3. **It must actually serve riders.** A shop qualifies when it rents bikes, sells or services
   electric bikes, or runs guided rides. Businesses that only list bike rental as a side category,
   such as a restaurant, are taken out.

We rank by Google star rating weighted against review count, so a shop with 4.9 stars across 800
reviews outranks a 5.0 from three reviews. A handful of reviews is not yet evidence. Rankings are
never paid: no business can buy a higher spot.

**Partner shops.** Shops can subscribe to [priority caller status](/business/) for their region. When
you call our rental line and choose a region, a partner shop in that region is rung first. That is the
only thing a subscription changes; it has no effect on listings or rankings.

We only say what a shop's public profile records. If a profile does not list a service, we do not
claim the shop offers it. That is why, for example, our battery page separates confirmed battery
specialists from repair shops you should call to check.

## What we are not

We are not a rental company. We do not own bikes, set shops' prices or hold deposits. When you send a
[Rent Now](/rent/) request, we find a shop that has what you need, send you a quote (the shop's price
plus our booking fee, shown separately) and, if you pay it, book the rental for you. If you contact a
shop directly instead, your agreement is with the shop, so confirm price, deposit, helmet policy,
minimum age and cancellation terms with them before you pay.

We do not write the reviews either. Star ratings and review counts are Google's, shown as data and
refreshed on a schedule. They can lag behind reality, so treat them as a starting point.

## Keeping it accurate

Shops move, change hours, get bought and close. Two things keep the directory current:

- **Regular re-imports.** The directory is rebuilt from a fresh data pull, which removes closed
  businesses and adds new ones.
- **You.** If a listing is wrong, out of date or should not be here, tell us on the
  [contact page](/contact/) and we will correct or remove it. Shop owners can ask for changes to
  their listing the same way, free of charge.

## How the site pays for itself

Florida Ebike Rentals is free to use and free to be listed in. It is supported by subscriptions from
partner shops for priority caller status, by clearly marked advertising, and by affiliate links: we earn a commission on qualifying Amazon purchases and on tours
booked through Viator. None of that affects which shops are listed or how they are ranked. The full
detail is in our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/) and [privacy policy](/privacy/).
