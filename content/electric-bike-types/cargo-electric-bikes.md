A cargo electric bike is built to carry things: children, beach chairs, a cooler, a week of groceries.
Its frame is longer and stronger than a normal bike's, and the motor makes a heavy load feel light. For a
family on holiday in a Florida beach town, it can replace the car for the trip to the beach.

## What is a cargo electric bike?

There are two main designs.

**Longtail cargo bikes** stretch the back of the bike, behind the rider, into a long, strong deck. The
deck can hold child seats, padded seats for older children, panniers or crates. A longtail rides much like
a normal bike, just longer.

**Front-loader cargo bikes,** sometimes called box bikes, put a large box or platform between the rider
and a front wheel set further forward. Children sit in the box facing forward, where you can see them.
Front-loaders carry more but steer differently, which takes some getting used to.

There are also **cargo trikes** with two wheels and a box at the front or back, which stand up on their
own. Whatever the design, cargo e-bikes have strong brakes, sturdy kickstands and a motor sized to move a
heavy load.

## How a cargo e-bike handles

A cargo bike is longer and heavier than anything else in this series, often 60 to 90 pounds before you
add the load. That affects everything:

- **Turning** takes a wider arc; plan your turns early.
- **Stopping** takes longer when the bike is loaded. Brake earlier than you would on a normal bike.
- **Starting and stopping** with children aboard is easiest from a sturdy centre stand, which most cargo
  bikes have. Load and unload with the bike on its stand.
- **Balance** is helped by keeping the heaviest load low and central.

The motor is what makes all this practical. A loaded cargo bike without assist is very hard work; with it,
the load almost disappears.

## Carrying children in Florida

Florida's helmet law applies to e-bikes: **riders and passengers under 16 must wear a properly fitted
helmet,** including a child in a seat. Our [Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/) has the
detail. Beyond the law:

- **Use proper child seats or the bike's built-in seating,** with belts done up, never a child sitting
  loose on a rack.
- **Check the bike's load limit** and the seat's age and weight limits with the shop.
- **Fit foot guards or wheel guards** so small feet stay out of the spokes.
- **Practise without the children first,** in a quiet car park or side street.

Our [family e-bike rental guide](/blog/family-ebike-rentals-florida/) covers child seats, trailers and
which towns suit families best, and the [family-friendly shops](/find/family-friendly/) page lists shops
that say they are good for kids.

## Where a cargo e-bike makes sense in Florida

Cargo e-bikes are best on flat, paved routes with somewhere to park a long bike at the end:

- **30A and the Emerald Coast,** where the [Timpoochee Trail](/trails/timpoochee-trail-30a/) links rental
  houses to beach accesses. Our [30A guide](/blog/30a-ebike-rentals-guide/) covers it.
- **Beach towns where you are staying near the sand,** carrying chairs, a cooler and an umbrella.
- **Town centres** where you would otherwise make several trips by car.

They are less suited to narrow, crowded paths and anywhere you might have to lift the bike.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| Carries children and gear in one trip | Heavy and long: hard to turn and park |
| Can replace a car for local trips | Longer stopping distances when loaded |

## What to ask the shop

1. **Is it a longtail or a front-loader, and can you show me how it handles?**
2. **What is the maximum load, including riders and passengers?**
3. **Which child seats fit, and are helmets for children included?**
4. **Does it deliver?** A cargo bike is awkward to collect. See our

## FAQs

### Can I carry my kids on a cargo e-bike in Florida?

Yes, in proper child seats or the bike's built-in seating. Riders and passengers under 16 must wear a
helmet under Florida law. Check the bike's load limit and the seat's age and weight limits with the shop.

### Is a cargo e-bike hard to ride?

A longtail rides much like a normal bike, just longer. A front-loader steers differently and takes a few
minutes to get used to. Both stop and turn more slowly when loaded, so practise empty first.

### How much can a cargo e-bike carry?

It depends on the model, and the limit includes the rider. Ask the shop for the exact figure before you
load up children and gear.

### Is a cargo bike better than a trailer?

A cargo bike keeps children closer and in view, and handles as one vehicle. A trailer can be added to an
ordinary bike. Our [family rental guide](/blog/family-ebike-rentals-florida/) compares the options.
