## Riding a rented bike in St. Augustine

St. Augustine packs a historic district with almost no parking, a bridge to a barrier island and —
southwest of town — a paved state rail trail toward Palatka. The visitor ride most people picture is
downtown early, then across the Bridge of Lions to Anastasia Island for the lighthouse and beach
access. An e-bike earns its keep on the bridge and on longer island loops.

The paved day ride outside town is the
[Palatka-to-St. Augustine State Trail](/trails/palatka-to-st-augustine-state-trail/): about 19 miles
of asphalt from Vermont Heights / Elkton west through Armstrong and Hastings to East River Road near
East Palatka. It does not reach the historic district — rent here, then drive to a St. Johns County
trailhead (Vermont Heights or Armstrong Park have parking and restrooms).

From late November through mid-January, [Nights of Lights](/blog/st-augustine-nights-of-lights-by-bike/)
changes the downtown plan: St. George Street, the Plaza interior and parts of the bayfront are
no-ride zones, so lock up at the edge and walk the lights. Ask the shop about evening return times
and lights before you book a night ride.

Florida treats e-bikes as bicycles on the road. Confirm Class 1–3 rules and any local sidewalk limits
with the shop; our [Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/) covers the statewide
baseline. A step-through cruiser or
[commuter e-bike](/electric-bike-types/commuter-electric-bikes/) suits old-town streets and the rail
trail; fat-tire beach bikes are optional on Anastasia Island sand, not required for the paved
corridor.

## FAQs

### Where should I ride an e-bike in St. Augustine?

Old town roads early, the Bridge of Lions to Anastasia Island for beach and lighthouse access, and
— for a longer paved day — the
[Palatka-to-St. Augustine State Trail](/trails/palatka-to-st-augustine-state-trail/) from Vermont
Heights or Armstrong Park. In Nights of Lights season, ride to the district edge and walk the no-ride
zones.

### Are e-bikes allowed on the Palatka-to-St. Augustine State Trail?

Yes. Florida State Parks allows electric bicycles on trails designated for bicycle use, consistent
with Section 316.20655 of Florida law. The trail starts southwest of town, not in the historic
district.

### What kind of bike should I rent in St. Augustine?

A Class 1 or Class 2 cruiser or commuter for downtown, the bridge and the state trail. Confirm
battery range if you plan the roughly 34-mile out-and-back from Vermont Heights to East River Road.
