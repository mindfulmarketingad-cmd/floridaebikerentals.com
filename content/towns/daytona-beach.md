## Riding a rented bike in Daytona Beach

Daytona Beach is one of the few places in Florida where the beach itself is the ride. The sand here
is fine and packs down hard, which is why Volusia County lets cars drive on marked stretches of it,
and why a fat-tire bike can cruise the waterline at low tide for miles. Check the tide before you set
off: the firm, wet sand near the water is the good riding surface, and it narrows sharply at high tide.

Fat-tire e-bikes are the popular rental here for exactly that reason. Their wide, low-pressure tyres
float over softer sand where a standard bike wheel digs in, and the motor takes the work out of a
headwind off the Atlantic. If you plan to stay on paved roads and the boardwalk, a step-through
cruiser is lighter and easier to park.

Away from the sand, the classic ride heads north to Ormond Beach and the Ormond Scenic Loop, a
canopied road through hammock and along the Halifax River. South of the city, Daytona Beach Shores
and Port Orange are an easy run along the coast.

Two weeks of the year change everything: Bike Week in March and Biketoberfest in October bring huge
numbers of motorcycles into town. Rentals book up and the main roads are loud and busy, so reserve
early and stick to side streets and the beach.

Rules for anything with a motor on the sand are set by Volusia County and do change, so ask the
shop what currently applies before you ride an e-bike on the beach.

## FAQs

### Can you ride a bike on Daytona Beach?

Yes. Daytona's hard-packed sand is firm enough that the county allows cars on marked sections, and
bicycles are a common sight along the waterline. Ride the firm, wet sand near the water at low tide.
For e-bikes specifically, ask your rental shop what Volusia County currently permits on the sand.

### What kind of bike should I rent for Daytona Beach?

A fat-tire bike if you want to ride on the beach: the wide tyres stay on top of the sand. A
step-through cruiser if you are sticking to roads, the boardwalk and the riverfront. An e-bike of
either kind makes the Atlantic headwind a non-issue.

### When is it hardest to rent a bike in Daytona Beach?

During Bike Week in March and Biketoberfest in October, when the city fills with motorcycle
visitors, and on summer holiday weekends. Book ahead for any of those dates.
