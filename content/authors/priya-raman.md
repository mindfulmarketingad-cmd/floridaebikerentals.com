Priya writes the family side of this directory. She has rented e-bikes in Florida with two children
under eight in most configurations available: trailers, rear seats, tag-alongs and a long-tail cargo
bike that she still argues is the only sensible answer.

Her guides focus on the things that decide whether a family ride is fun or a disaster: minimum ages
that vary by shop rather than by law, helmet fit, how far children will actually ride before they
want ice cream, and which routes let you turn back early.
