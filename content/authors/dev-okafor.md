Dev has ridden the Pinellas Trail end to end more times than is reasonable, and has covered most of
Florida's paved rail-trail network on a mix of pedal and electric bikes. He writes the trail guides
on this site: where a route actually starts, where the shade is, which sections are worth the drive,
and which rental town puts you closest to the good part.

Before this he spent six years leading guided rides, which is where his low tolerance for vague
route descriptions comes from. If a guide here says a trail is nineteen miles, it is because he
measured it.
