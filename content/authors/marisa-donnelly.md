Marisa built Florida Ebike Rentals after one badly researched family holiday on 30A, where three
of the four rental shops she called had either moved or stopped renting e-bikes. She now maintains
the directory's data pipeline, decides what qualifies as a rental partner, and handles the
corrections that shop owners and readers send in.

She covers the parts of this site where accuracy matters most: how listings are chosen and ranked,
what Florida's e-bike statutes actually say, and how rental pricing behaves across the state's
seasons. She rides a Class 2 step-through and is unapologetic about the throttle.
