The guides on this site are researched and edited by the team that runs Florida Ebike Rentals.

**Where the facts come from.** Rental listings are built from each business's public Google
Business profile and refreshed periodically. E-bike rules come from the Florida Statutes and from
city and county ordinances. Trail distances, surfaces and access points come from the agencies that
manage each trail, cross-checked against published route maps. Prices come from shops' own published
rates, and every guide says when its figures were last checked.

**What we do not do.** We do not take payment for placement in the rankings, and the rankings are
calculated the same way for every shop: Google star rating weighted by review count. Where a page
earns a commission — tour bookings through Viator, products on Amazon — it says so next to the link.

**Corrections.** Rules, hours and prices change. If something on a page is wrong, use the
[contact page](/contact/) and we will check it and update the page.
