## What makes a bike an e-bike

An electric bicycle is a bicycle with a small electric motor and a battery that help you along. Under
Florida law it is still treated as a bicycle as long as it has working pedals, a motor of 750 watts or
less, and a top assisted speed that fits one of the three classes: 20 mph for Class 1 and Class 2, and
28 mph for Class 3. Our guide to [e-bike classes](/blog/ebike-classes-explained/) explains the
difference between pedal assist and a throttle, and our [Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/)
covers helmets, ages and where you can ride.

The class tells you how the motor behaves. The **type**, which is what this hub is about, tells you how
the bike is built: the frame, the wheels, the tires and what it is designed to carry. Two e-bikes of the
same class can feel completely different to ride if one is a heavy fat-tire bike and the other is a
light commuter.

## The nine types you will meet in Florida

- **[Cruiser electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/cruiser-electric-bikes/)**: upright, comfortable and
  relaxed. The most common e-bike at Florida rental counters.
- **[Beach cruisers](/electric-bike-types/beach-cruiser/)**: the classic beach-town bike, with or without
  a motor, built for flat seaside paths.
- **[Fat-tire electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/fat-tire-electric-bikes/)**: tires about four inches
  wide for sand, shell and rough ground.
- **[Step-through electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/step-through-electric-bikes/)**: a low frame you
  can walk through instead of swinging a leg over.
- **[Commuter electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/commuter-electric-bikes/)**: quicker, lighter
  city bikes with lights, fenders and racks.
- **[Folding electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/folding-electric-bikes/)**: small wheels and a hinged
  frame that packs into a car trunk, RV or boat.
- **[Cargo electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/cargo-electric-bikes/)**: long frames that carry
  children, shopping or beach gear.
- **[Electric mountain bikes](/electric-bike-types/electric-mountain-bikes/)**: suspension and knobby
  tires for off-road trails.
- **[Lightweight electric bikes](/electric-bike-types/lightweight-electric-bikes/)**: slim frames with
  small batteries that you can lift and carry.

## How to choose a type for your trip

Start with where you will ride, not with the bike.

**Beach towns with flat paved paths.** On 30A, in Key West, around St. Augustine or along the Gulf
beaches, almost everyone is best served by a cruiser or a step-through. Comfort matters more than speed
when the ride is a few miles to the beach and back. Our [30A guide](/blog/30a-ebike-rentals-guide/) and
[Key West guide](/blog/key-west-ebike-guide/) cover those towns.

**Sand and rough ground.** If you plan to ride on packed sand where riding is allowed, or on shell and
dirt tracks, a fat-tire bike floats where narrow tires dig in.

**City riding and long paved trails.** For getting around Orlando, St. Petersburg or Miami, or for
covering distance on trails like the [Pinellas Trail](/trails/pinellas-trail/) or the
[West Orange Trail](/trails/west-orange-trail/), a commuter or hybrid-style e-bike is quicker and less
tiring over many miles.

**Families.** A cargo bike can carry small children, and step-through frames make it easy for
everyone to get on and off. Read our [family e-bike rental guide](/blog/family-ebike-rentals-florida/)
before you book.

**Travel and storage.** If the bike has to fit in a car, an RV, a boat or a small condo, look at folding
or lightweight e-bikes.

## What rental shops usually stock

Most Florida rental fleets are built around comfortable cruiser and step-through e-bikes, because they
suit the widest range of riders on flat, paved beach paths. Fat-tire bikes are common in beach towns too.
Cargo, folding and mountain e-bikes are less common in rental fleets and are worth asking about before
you travel.

You choose a type of bike, but the exact make and model is always up to the shop. Our
[e-bike finder](/#finder) shows the shops that name your style in their listing first, then the other
e-bike shops worth asking. Call to confirm the bike is free before you go.

## FAQs

### What is the most common type of e-bike to rent in Florida?

Cruiser and step-through e-bikes. They are comfortable, easy to ride and suited to the flat paved paths
in Florida's beach towns, which is where most rentals happen.

### Which type of e-bike is best for the beach?

On paved beach paths, a cruiser or beach cruiser. On packed sand where riding is allowed, a fat-tire
e-bike. Check local rules before riding on any beach: many do not allow bikes on the sand at all.

### Is the type of e-bike the same as its class?

No. The class (1, 2 or 3) describes how the motor assists you and its top assisted speed. The type
describes how the bike is built. Any type can come in any class.

### Do I need a licence to ride any of these e-bikes in Florida?

No. An electric bicycle that meets Florida's definition, with working pedals, a motor of 750 watts or
less and a top assisted speed within its class, is treated as a bicycle and needs no licence,
registration or insurance. Shops set their own minimum ages.

### Can I choose the exact e-bike I rent?

You can choose the type. The exact make and model depends on the shop's fleet. A Rent Now request lets
you pick a preferred style, and your quote tells you what the shop has before you pay.
