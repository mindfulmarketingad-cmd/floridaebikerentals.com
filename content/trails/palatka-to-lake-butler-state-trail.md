The Palatka to Lake Butler State Trail is a 47-mile former Norfolk Southern rail corridor running
west and north-west from Palatka on the St. Johns River, through Florahome and Keystone Heights
country, toward Lake Butler in Union County.

It is one of the longest single corridors in the Florida state trail system and one of the least
developed. A substantial paved section is open at the Palatka end; much of the rest of the corridor
is in state ownership but not yet surfaced for road-tyre riding.

## What is actually rideable

Treat the Palatka end as the trail and the rest as a project. The paved section running west out of
Palatka toward the Florahome area is the part worth driving to, and it is genuinely good riding:
quiet, straight, shaded in places, and almost empty on a weekday.

Beyond the paved section the corridor continues on varied unsurfaced ground. Some of it is rideable
on a fat-tyre bike, some of it is not. Check the current status with Florida State Parks or the
Office of Greenways and Trails before assuming you can ride to Lake Butler.

## The route

Palatka is the hub. The trail begins near the town's riverfront trail junction, where it shares a
starting area with the
[Palatka to St. Augustine State Trail](/trails/palatka-to-st-augustine-state-trail/) heading the
other way.

From there it runs west through Putnam County pine woods and small settlements. There are few road
crossings, almost no services, and long straight stretches on the old rail alignment.

## What it is like to ride

Peaceful, and empty. This is not a destination trail with a café at the end. It is quiet north
Florida backcountry on good asphalt, with pine, oak, wetland and pasture, and it suits riders who
want distance and solitude rather than scenery and stops.

Palatka at the eastern end is the practical base: parking, food, water, restrooms, and the junction
with the St. Augustine corridor if you want to ride both in a day.

## Trailheads and parking

Free parking at the Palatka trailhead and at the marked state trail access points along the paved
section. Facilities are minimal beyond Palatka - assume no water on the trail.

## Surface, shade and conditions

- **Asphalt** on the developed section, in good condition.
- **Mixed shade**, better in the wooded stretches near Palatka.
- **Very few road crossings.**
- **No services** once you leave town.
- **Unsurfaced corridor** beyond the paved end. Do not plan a road-tyre ride onto it.

## Why an e-bike suits this trail

Distance is the whole proposition here, and heat is the limiting factor rather than terrain - the
exact problem assist solves. A 25 or 30-mile out-and-back through empty Putnam County is a plausible
morning on an e-bike and a hard day pedalling in August. Assist also lets you ride out from Palatka
on this corridor and back on the St. Augustine corridor without picking one.

Class 1 and Class 2 are standard rentals and generally accepted on Florida state trails; Class 3
rules vary by managing authority. See our [e-bike classes guide](/blog/ebike-classes-explained/) and
the [Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/).

## FAQs

### How long is the Palatka to Lake Butler State Trail?

The corridor is 47 miles, from Palatka in Putnam County north-west toward Lake Butler in Union
County. Only part of it is paved; the paved section is at the Palatka end.

### Can you ride the whole Palatka to Lake Butler trail?

Not on a road-tyre bike. The developed paved section runs west from Palatka; the rest of the corridor
is in state ownership but not fully surfaced. Check current status with Florida State Parks.

### Where does the Palatka to Lake Butler State Trail start?

At the Palatka end, near the riverfront trail junction it shares with the Palatka to St. Augustine
State Trail.

### Can you ride an e-bike on the Palatka to Lake Butler State Trail?

Yes. Class 1 and Class 2 are the usual rentals and are generally accepted on Florida state trails.
Confirm the position on Class 3 before riding.

### Is there water or food on the trail?

No. Palatka is the only reliable place for either. Carry everything you need.

### Where can I rent an e-bike near Palatka?

Rental provision in Palatka itself is limited. St. Augustine and Gainesville are the nearest clusters,
and several shops in the region deliver.

## The short version

Park in Palatka, ride west on the paved section, and turn around before the asphalt runs out. Check
the current build status first, carry your own water, and use Palatka as the base for both state
trail corridors that meet there.
