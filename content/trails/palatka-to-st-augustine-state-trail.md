The Palatka to St. Augustine State Trail is a roughly 19-mile corridor running north-east from
Palatka on the St. Johns River across Putnam and St. Johns counties toward St. Augustine, on the
route of an old Florida East Coast Railway branch line.

Like several Florida state trails it has been built in phases. Substantial paved sections are open
and ridable at both ends, with connecting work continuing in between - so check the current status
with Florida State Parks or the Office of Greenways and Trails before planning an end-to-end ride.

## The route

The Palatka end begins near the riverfront, where the trail also meets the corridor heading west
toward Lake Butler. From there it runs north-east through Putnam County pine and pasture, past the
Armstrong area, and on into St. Johns County toward St. Augustine.

The St. Augustine end approaches the country's oldest city through its western outskirts, which makes
this the only Florida state trail that finishes somewhere with genuine tourist infrastructure at one
end and a working river town at the other.

## What it is like to ride

Rural and quiet. Long straight sections, light traffic at crossings, pine woods and open farmland,
and very few other riders on a weekday. It is not a scenic trail in the way the Keys or the Nature
Coast are; it is a calm, easy, well-surfaced ride through the parts of north Florida people drive
past.

Palatka itself is worth an hour: the riverfront on the St. Johns, a small downtown, and the junction
of two state trail corridors.

## Trailheads and parking

Parking is at the state trail trailheads at the Palatka end, the Armstrong area in the middle, and
the St. Johns County end toward St. Augustine. All are free. Facilities are basic - assume no water
beyond what you carry.

For a first ride, start in Palatka and ride north-east. It is the easiest section to find, the
easiest to park at, and it puts a town with food at the end of your return leg.

## Surface, shade and conditions

- **Asphalt** on the completed sections, generally in good condition.
- **Patchy shade** - better in the wooded Putnam County stretches, thin elsewhere.
- **Few road crossings**, mostly rural.
- **No services** between trailheads.
- **Summer storms** build fast; there is nowhere to shelter.

## Why an e-bike suits this trail

The appeal here is distance without difficulty, and that is what assist delivers. A 30-mile
out-and-back through quiet country is a full day pedalling in north Florida humidity and a relaxed
morning on an e-bike. It also opens up the option of riding from Palatka and continuing onto the
[Palatka to Lake Butler State Trail](/trails/palatka-to-lake-butler-state-trail/) rather than
choosing between them.

Class 1 and Class 2 are the standard rentals and the classes generally accepted on Florida state
trails; Class 3 rules vary by managing authority. See our
[e-bike classes guide](/blog/ebike-classes-explained/) and the
[Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/).

## Practical notes

- **Check what is open** before you drive. This corridor is still being completed in places.
- **Carry water and a repair kit.** There is nothing between trailheads.
- **Rentals** are in St. Augustine and Jacksonville, not on the trail. Palatka has one local option
  and several St. Augustine shops deliver.
- **Combine it with St. Augustine** - the historic district is a good afternoon on a bike in its own
  right.

## FAQs

### How long is the Palatka to St. Augustine State Trail?

The corridor is about 19 miles, running north-east from Palatka toward St. Augustine across Putnam
and St. Johns counties.

### Is the Palatka to St. Augustine State Trail complete?

Not entirely. It has been built in phases, with paved sections open and connecting work continuing.
Florida State Parks and the Office of Greenways and Trails publish the current status.

### Where can I park for the Palatka to St. Augustine State Trail?

There are free state trail trailheads at the Palatka end, in the Armstrong area, and at the St. Johns
County end. Facilities are basic.

### Can you ride an e-bike on the Palatka to St. Augustine State Trail?

Yes. Class 1 and Class 2 e-bikes are the usual rentals and are generally accepted on Florida's state
trails. Confirm the current position on Class 3 before riding.

### Does this trail connect to other trails?

Yes, at Palatka, where it meets the corridor of the Palatka to Lake Butler State Trail heading west.
Together they make Palatka a genuine trail-town hub.

### Where can I rent an e-bike for this trail?

St. Augustine has the most shops within easy reach, with Jacksonville not much further. There is
limited rental provision in Palatka itself, so book ahead or arrange delivery.

## The short version

Start in Palatka, ride north-east into Putnam County, and check the current build status before you
commit to reaching St. Augustine on the trail. Quiet, flat and easy - the kind of ride an e-bike
makes twice as long for no extra effort.
