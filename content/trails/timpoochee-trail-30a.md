The Timpoochee Trail is the reason 30A has more e-bike rental shops per mile than anywhere else in
Florida. It runs the full length of County Road 30A, from Dune Allen in the west to Inlet Beach in
the east, connecting a string of beach towns spaced almost exactly one snack apart.

## Route at a glance

The trail parallels 30A for roughly 19 miles of continuous paved path. It is flat, wide enough for
two-abreast riding through most sections, and rarely more than a few hundred yards from a beach
access, a restroom or a coffee shop.

Riding it one way takes two to three hours with stops on an e-bike. Most visitors ride sections
instead, using a town as a base and turning back after lunch.

## The towns, west to east

- **Dune Allen** - the quiet western end, and the best place to start with nervous riders.
- **Gulf Place and Santa Rosa Beach** - the highest concentration of rental shops, restaurants and
  public parking. The practical base for most visitors.
- **Blue Mountain Beach** - the closest thing to a climb on 30A, which is to say a gentle rise, plus
  one of the best ice cream stops on the route.
- **Grayton Beach** - sandy side streets, old Florida character, and the entrance to Grayton Beach
  State Park.
- **WaterColor and Seaside** - the postcard stretch. Seaside's central square is the single busiest
  point on the whole trail; walk the bike through it in season.
- **Seagrove Beach** - quieter, with good beach accesses and fewer crowds.
- **Seacrest and Alys Beach** - the white-architecture stretch, immaculate verges, excellent surface.
- **Rosemary Beach** - cobbled streets and the eastern anchor town.
- **Inlet Beach** - the eastern end, with access on toward Camp Helen State Park.

## The classic half day

Rent in Santa Rosa Beach, ride east to Seaside for lunch, carry on to Rosemary Beach for coffee, then
ride back. That is roughly 20 miles round trip - a long day on a beach cruiser and an easy afternoon
on an e-bike. It is the ride that converts people.

For a shorter first outing, Dune Allen to Grayton Beach and back is about 8 miles with the calmest
traffic crossings on the corridor.

## Surface and conditions

The surface is asphalt throughout, generally in good condition, with concrete sections through the
town centres. Two things to watch:

- **Sand drift.** After wind, the path picks up sand near the western dune crossings. It behaves
  like gravel under a narrow tyre; fat tyres barely notice.
- **Driveway crossings.** The trail crosses residential driveways and side streets constantly, and
  golf carts pull out of them without much ceremony.

Shade is limited. The scrub oak sections offer patches, but most of the trail is exposed. In July
and August, ride before 10am or after 5pm.

## Parking and trailheads

Public parking on 30A fills early and stays full. The reliable options are the larger public beach
accesses at Dune Allen, Gulf Place, Grayton Beach and Inlet Beach, plus the state park lots. In peak
weeks, riding from your rental house is genuinely faster than driving to a trailhead - which is the
whole argument for renting bikes for the week rather than by the day.

## Riding it safely

Most of the traffic on the Timpoochee Trail is other people on holiday, many on an e-bike for the
first time. Pass on the left with a call or a bell, keep your speed sensible through the town
centres, and expect children to change direction without warning.

Cross 30A only at marked crossings. Traffic moves faster than the surroundings suggest, and dune
vegetation makes sight lines poor.

## FAQs

### How long is the Timpoochee Trail?

About 19 miles of continuous paved path running the length of County Road 30A between Dune Allen and
Inlet Beach. Riding the full length one way takes two to three hours on an e-bike with stops.

### Are e-bikes allowed on the Timpoochee Trail?

Class 1 and Class 2 e-bikes are widely used on the trail and are what every 30A rental shop hands
out. Local rules can change and are set locally rather than by the state, so confirm the current
position with the shop you rent from - it is the question they answer most.

### Where is the best place to rent an e-bike for the Timpoochee Trail?

Santa Rosa Beach has the deepest choice of shops and sits mid-corridor, which keeps both directions
in easy reach. Most shops on 30A also deliver to rental houses, which removes the problem of moving
bikes in a hire car.

### Is the Timpoochee Trail good for young children?

The western end around Dune Allen is calm, flat and has the fewest crossings, which makes it the
best starting point. The Seaside and WaterColor stretch is the busiest and least suitable for
children riding their own bikes in peak season.

### Do I need a fat-tyre bike?

No. The trail is paved end to end. Fat tyres help only with the sand that drifts across the western
crossings, and with riding on hard-packed sand where that is permitted.
