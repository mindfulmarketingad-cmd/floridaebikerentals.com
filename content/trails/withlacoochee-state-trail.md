The Withlacoochee State Trail is the longest ride in this series and one of the longest paved rail
trails in Florida: about 46 miles of 12-foot-wide asphalt from Gulf Junction in Citrus Springs south
through Inverness, Floral City and the Withlacoochee State Forest to Owensboro Junction, six miles
north of Dade City. Florida State Parks gives the length as 46 or 47 miles depending on the page.

It follows the old Plant System line that once linked Croom and Inverness. CSX sold the 46-mile
corridor to the state in 1989, one of the first purchases under Florida's Rails to Trails programme,
and it is now a National Recreation Trail. That railroad heritage makes it flat and gently graded the
whole way, which is what makes it such a good e-bike ride: the distance is the only challenge, and
assist takes care of most of that.

November to April is the season to ride it. The trail runs through open ranch land and forest well
inland from the coast, and the cooler, drier months are far more comfortable than summer.

## Route at a glance

The trail crosses three counties: Citrus, Hernando and Pasco. There are mile markers along the way,
plus surviving railroad cement mileage and whistle markers. An unpaved equestrian trail runs beside
the pavement on parts of the route; that is for horses, not bikes.

Approximate distances from the southern end, from the [Florida Hikes mile log](https://floridahikes.com/withlacoochee-state-trail/)
(which measures about 44 miles of trail), give a feel for how the towns are spaced:

- **Dade City end (0) and Ridge Manor trailhead (2.4).** The southern start, near the I-75 / SR 50
  interchange.
- **Croom area (around 8 to 9).** The trail runs through the Withlacoochee State Forest here, the
  most wooded and remote-feeling section.
- **Istachatta (14.3) and Floral City (21.3).** Small trail towns about seven miles apart.
- **Fort Cooper State Park back entrance (25.4).** A side trip to the park's lake and nature trails.
- **Inverness (about 28).** Wallace Brooks Park on Lake Henderson, and the town's historic depot,
  built in 1892 and now privately owned. The trail's office is in Inverness.
- **Hernando trailhead (33.3), Citrus Springs (39.7) and Gulf Junction (44.1).** The quieter northern
  end.

Five trailheads have parking and restrooms: Gulf Junction, South Citrus Springs, Inverness, Ridge
Manor and Owensboro. Gulf Junction, Inverness and Ridge Manor also have picnic areas, and there are
benches and covered shelters at intervals along the trail. Florida State Parks publishes the
[official trail map](https://www.floridastateparks.org/sites/default/files/media/file/Withlacoochee%20State%20Trail-070716-web.pdf).

## The sections worth riding

**Inverness to Floral City and back (about 14 miles).** The pick for a first ride. Start in downtown
Inverness by Lake Henderson, where the trail's railroad trestle stands, and ride south to Floral City
for a break before turning around. It is the section with the most to see and the easiest logistics, since the Inverness shops
are right in town.

**Floral City to Istachatta and on into the forest.** The rural middle of the trail, with long quiet
runs through ranch land and the Withlacoochee State Forest. Services are thinner here, so carry
water and start with a full battery.

**Ridge Manor to Croom.** The southern end, handy if you are coming from Tampa or Orlando on I-75. The
Croom area of the state forest has its own unpaved trails for anyone on a mountain-style e-bike.

**The whole thing.** 46 miles one way is a big day even on assist, and an out-and-back is beyond most
rental batteries. If you want to ride end to end, plan a car shuttle, or split it over two days with a
night in Inverness. Ask your rental shop what range to expect before you set off.

## Rules on the trail

- **E-bikes are allowed.** Florida State Parks says bicycles, including electric bicycles, may use
  trails designated for bicycle use, consistent with Section 316.20655 of Florida law. Some older
  third-party guides still say otherwise; the park's own
  [experiences page](https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/withlacoochee-state-trail/experiences-amenities)
  is the current word.
- **Obey posted speed limits** and ride at a responsible speed. Drop the assist level near walkers;
  our [e-bike classes guide](/blog/ebike-classes-explained/) explains what each class can do.
- **Cyclists yield to other users, and everyone yields to horses.** Keep right, pass on the left and
  give a clear warning when you pass.
- **Hours are 8 a.m. to sunset**, 365 days a year, and there is no fee.
- **Dogs are welcome** on a leash no longer than six feet.
- **Watch the road crossings** and obey traffic signals.

Florida law requires helmets for riders under 16. Our [Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/)
covers the rest of the state rules.

## Renting for the Withlacoochee State Trail

Inverness is the natural base. Two shops on North Pine Avenue in downtown Inverness, a few blocks from
the trail, are in our directory: **Trek Bicycle Inverness**, which lists electric bikes and rentals
with online booking, and **Sparky's Green Rides**, which offers rentals and repairs. Call Sparky's to
ask whether e-bikes are available.

In Hernando County, **E Cycle Adventures** in Brooksville lists e-bike rentals, guided tours and
delivery, and **Crank Works Bicycles - Good Neighbor Rentals** is also in Brooksville. North of the
trail, **Electric Scooters & Bikes** in Dunnellon rents e-bikes through an online booking page.

## FAQs

### How long is the Withlacoochee State Trail?

About 46 miles from Gulf Junction in Citrus Springs to Owensboro Junction near Dade City. Florida
State Parks quotes 46 or 47 miles on different pages.

### Are e-bikes allowed on the Withlacoochee State Trail?

Yes. Florida State Parks allows electric bicycles on trails designated for bicycle use, consistent
with Section 316.20655 of Florida law. Obey posted speed limits and yield to walkers and horses.

### Where is the best place to start the Withlacoochee State Trail?

Inverness, for the most scenic section, Lake Henderson and nearby rentals. Ridge Manor is
the easiest start from I-75, and Gulf Junction is the quiet northern end.

### What are the trail's hours, and is there a fee?

The trail is open 8 a.m. to sunset every day, and there is no fee.

### Is there parking on the Withlacoochee State Trail?

Yes. Gulf Junction, South Citrus Springs, Inverness, Ridge Manor and Owensboro trailheads all have
parking and restrooms.

### Can you ride the whole trail in a day on an e-bike?

One way, yes, if your battery has the range and you arrange a shuttle back. An out-and-back of more
than 90 miles is beyond most rental e-bikes, so most riders pick one section.

## The short version

Rent in Inverness, start after 8 a.m., ride south to Floral City and back, and save the forest sections for a second day. It is the best long, quiet, flat ride in Central
Florida, and late autumn through spring is the time to do it.

Confirm opening hours with the shop before you drive over.
