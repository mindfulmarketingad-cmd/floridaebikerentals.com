The Good Neighbor Trail runs about ten miles east from downtown Brooksville out to the Withlacoochee
State Trail near Ridge Manor, following an old rail corridor across the rolling country of Hernando
County.

It is short, and it is the connector that makes the Withlacoochee reachable from a town with parking,
coffee and somewhere to eat. It also has something almost no other Florida rail trail can offer:
noticeable, sustained gradient.

## The route

The western trailhead is in downtown Brooksville, near Russell Street. From there the trail climbs
gently out of town - Brooksville sits on the Brooksville Ridge, one of the higher points in
peninsular Florida - then settles into a long, steady descent east through pasture, oak and pine to
the Withlacoochee State Trail crossing near Ridge Manor.

Ride it east and you get a downhill run out and a climb home. Plan for that.

## What it is good for

Two things. It is a pleasant ten-mile ride on its own, and it is the access route to the
Withlacoochee State Trail, which at roughly 46 miles is the longest paved rail trail in Florida. Ride
out on the Good Neighbor, turn north or south on the Withlacoochee, and you can make a day of
whatever length you like.

Downtown Brooksville at the western end is a genuine small Florida town centre rather than a car
park, which makes it a better base than the Withlacoochee's own trailheads.

## Surface, shade and conditions

Paved for its length, with a mix of open and shaded sections. Expect:

- **Gradient** - modest by any standard outside Florida, real by Florida's. The return to Brooksville
  is an actual climb.
- **Rural crossings** at road junctions, most with good sight lines.
- **Patchy shade**, better on the eastern half.
- **Very light use** on weekdays. You may have long stretches to yourself.

Hernando County is inland and hot. From May to September, start early.

## Why an e-bike suits this trail

This is the Florida trail where pedal assist buys you something other than heat tolerance: the ride
back into Brooksville is uphill after you have already done the miles. On a pedal bike that shapes
how far east you dare to go. On an e-bike it does not, which is precisely what lets you turn onto the
Withlacoochee and keep going.

Class 1 and Class 2 bikes are the standard rentals and the classes generally accepted on shared-use
paths and Florida state trails. Class 3 rules are set by the managing authority - see our
[e-bike classes guide](/blog/ebike-classes-explained/) and the
[Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/).

## Practical notes

- **Park in downtown Brooksville** and use the town for food, water and restrooms before you start.
- **Water is scarce** on the trail itself. Carry it.
- **The Withlacoochee connection** at the eastern end is the reason to come. Decide before you set
  off whether you are turning onto it, because the extra miles are on top of the climb home.
- **Rentals** are not on the trail. Nearest options are the north Tampa Bay coastal towns; several
  shops deliver.

## FAQs

### How long is the Good Neighbor Trail?

Roughly ten miles from downtown Brooksville east to the Withlacoochee State Trail near Ridge Manor.

### Where does the Good Neighbor Trail start?

At the western trailhead in downtown Brooksville, near Russell Street. The eastern end meets the
Withlacoochee State Trail.

### Is the Good Neighbor Trail hilly?

By Florida standards, yes. Brooksville sits on the Brooksville Ridge, so the trail descends heading
east and climbs on the way back. It is gentle but sustained, and noticeable at the end of a ride.

### Does the Good Neighbor Trail connect to the Withlacoochee State Trail?

Yes - that connection is its main purpose. The Withlacoochee is around 46 miles and the longest
paved rail trail in the state.

### Can you ride an e-bike on the Good Neighbor Trail?

E-bikes are commonly ridden here and on the connecting Withlacoochee State Trail. Class 1 and Class 2
are the usual rentals; check the current position on Class 3 with Hernando County or the state trail
office.

### Where can I rent an e-bike near Brooksville?

There is no shop directly on the trail. The nearest rental cluster is the northern Tampa Bay coast
around Hudson and New Port Richey, and several Tampa-area shops deliver.

## The short version

Park in downtown Brooksville, ride east to the Withlacoochee, and decide there how big a day you
want. Save enough battery for the climb back into town - it is the one Florida trail where that
sentence means something.
