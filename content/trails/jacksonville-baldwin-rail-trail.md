The Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail Trail is Jacksonville's long paved ride: about 14.5 miles of 12-foot
asphalt on a former CSX railroad corridor, running west from Imeson Road near I-295 to the small
town of Baldwin and on to Brandy Branch Road. The City of Jacksonville bought the corridor in 1992
through the Florida Greenways and Trails Program, and its Parks and Recreation department runs it as
a city park. A separate unpaved equestrian path runs alongside the pavement.

It is a quieter ride than you would expect from a trail that starts about five miles from downtown.
Bike Florida describes a dense canopy over most of the route — in places it feels like a tunnel —
through pine flatwoods, wetlands and hardwood uplands, with a crossing of McGirts Creek near the
Civil War site at Camp Milton.

The trail is dead flat, so an e-bike is less about hills than about turning a 29-mile out-and-back
into an easy morning. Class 1 or Class 2 assist is plenty. Note the one rule that is specific to
Jacksonville: a 15 mph limit for e-bikes on shared-use paths (details below).

## Route at a glance

The City of Jacksonville lists six trailheads. From east to west:

- **Imeson Road (1800 Imeson Road, 32220).** The eastern end, just off Commonwealth Avenue west of I-295. Parking. The trail crosses Commonwealth Avenue at grade shortly after you start, and the first mile runs through the forested Bulls Bay Preserve.
- **Camp Milton Historic Preserve (1225 Halsema Road, 32220).** The city's mid-point trailhead, about 7 miles from Imeson Road. A short spur leads to the preserve, the site of a Civil War encampment on the rail line. A good base for a shorter ride in either direction.
- **Livestock Farm (1172 Halsema Road, 32220).** A second access point on Halsema Road next to Camp Milton.
- **Otis Road (1380 Otis Road, 32220).** West of Camp Milton, with parking.
- **Baldwin (850 Center Street North, 32234).** The old depot site at the north end of Center Street in Baldwin, by Larry M. Carroll Memorial Park. The trail passes under U.S. 301 on the way in.
- **Brandy Branch Road (89 Brandy Branch Road, 32234).** The western end at County Road 121, just past Baldwin.

Mile markers run 1 to 14 from east to west, which makes it easy to judge your turnaround. Bike
Florida says restrooms are at most trailheads and at Camp Milton, and sheltered benches sit along
the route at regular intervals. Near Camp Milton you may hear Navy aircraft training at Whitehouse
Field.

## The sections worth riding

**Imeson Road to Camp Milton and back (about 14 miles).** The classic first ride: Bulls Bay
Preserve at the start, steady shade, and the Camp Milton preserve as a turnaround stop.

**Camp Milton to Baldwin and back.** The more rural half, past Otis Road and into Baldwin. The
Timucuan Parks Foundation suggests riding on to Baldwin for lunch at one of the town's diners —
check what is open before you count on it.

**The whole trail, out and back.** The Bike Florida cue sheet puts the full out-and-back at about
28.6 miles. That is well within most rental e-bike batteries on low to moderate assist, but ask the
shop what range to expect and start with a full charge.

## Rules on the trail

- **E-bikes are allowed.** Florida law gives an e-bike the same rights and duties as a bicycle and lets it use bicycle and shared-use paths, while letting the agency in charge of a path restrict e-bikes ([section 316.20655](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/316.20655)). The City of Jacksonville's [e-bike rules page](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/planning-department/Transportation-Planning/Ped-Bike-Planning/Bicycle-and-Pedestrian-Advisory-Committee) does not list the rail trail as an exception; follow any posted signs.
- **15 mph maximum.** Under [Ordinance 2026-0570-E](https://jaxcityc.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8144675&GUID=F68F3489-B3BC-4928-9623-EBEDF858D392), approved by the Jacksonville City Council on September 22, 2026, e-bike riders may not exceed 15 mph on sidewalks or shared-use paths, and can still be cited for riding too fast for conditions.
- **Warn, pass wide and yield.** The ordinance requires an audible warning (bell or voice) when passing and a safe passing distance, and e-bike riders must yield to pedestrians. Expect walkers, skaters and, on the parallel path and at crossings, horses.
- **Ride no more than two abreast**, per Bike Florida's trail notes.
- **Hours.** The [City of Jacksonville](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/parks-and-recreation/jaxparks/all-parks/jacksonville-baldwin-rail-trail) lists the trail as open sunrise to sundown. The [Timucuan Parks Foundation](https://www.timucuanparks.org/parks/jacksonville-baldwin-rail-trail/) lists **no fee** for parking or access.
- **Leashed pets** are welcome.
- **Helmets** are required for riders and passengers under 16 under [Florida law](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/316.2065) and are a good idea for everyone.

Our [Florida e-bike law guide](/blog/florida-ebike-laws/) covers the statewide rules, and our

Riverwalk, the Emerald Trail and the rest of the city's ordinance.

## Renting for the Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail Trail

There is no rental desk on the trail. Rent in Jacksonville and drive to Imeson Road or Camp Milton,
and confirm with the shop that the bike can leave their area and that you can fit it in your car or
on a rack.

For e-bikes, **Art Bikes Jax** at 3544 St Johns Ave in Avondale is listed as an electric bicycle
store with rentals and guided tours (5.0 stars / 189 reviews), and **Beach E-Bikes Rentals & Sales**
on Atlantic Blvd in Neptune Beach is listed with e-bike rentals and delivery (4.9 stars / 59
reviews) — a long drive from the trail, so ask about delivery. Details:
[Art Bikes Jax](/cities/jacksonville/art-bikes-jax/) and
[Beach E-Bikes Rentals & Sales](/cities/jacksonville/beach-e-bikes-rentals-sales-jacksonville/).

For regular bikes, **Open Road Bicycles** at 4460 Hendricks Ave (4.9 stars / 818 reviews),
**Bicycles Etc.** at 8036 Philips Hwy (4.8 stars / 701 reviews), **ZenCog Bicycle Company** at 883
Stockton St (4.8 stars / 276 reviews) and **Tucker Cycles** at 3544 St Johns Ave (4.9 stars / 112
reviews) are all listed with rentals. Whether a given shop has e-bikes in its rental fleet changes,
so call ahead. Listings:

## FAQs

### How long is the Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail Trail?

About 14.5 miles one way, according to the City of Jacksonville (Bike Florida and TrailLink list
14.6), from Imeson Road in Jacksonville west to Brandy Branch Road just past Baldwin. Out and back
is roughly 29 miles.

### Are e-bikes allowed on the Jacksonville-Baldwin Rail Trail?

Florida law lets e-bikes use shared-use paths unless the managing agency restricts them, and the
City of Jacksonville's e-bike rules page does not list an exception for the trail. City Ordinance
2026-0570-E limits e-bikes to 15 mph on shared-use paths and requires riders to yield to
pedestrians. Obey any posted signs.

### Where is the best place to start?

Imeson Road for the full ride from the Jacksonville end. Camp Milton Historic Preserve on Halsema
Road for a mid-point start with a choice of direction. Baldwin or Brandy Branch Road if you are
coming from the west.

### What are the hours, and is there a fee?

The City of Jacksonville lists sunrise to sundown. The Timucuan Parks Foundation lists no fees for
parking or access.

### Is the trail shaded?

Mostly. Bike Florida describes dense tree canopy over most of the route, which makes it one of the
more comfortable long rides in Northeast Florida in warm weather. Still carry water: services are
limited to the trailheads and Baldwin.

## The short version

Rent in Jacksonville, park at Imeson Road or Camp Milton after sunrise, keep it under 15 mph, ring
before you pass, and ride at least to Camp Milton and back. Late autumn through spring is the best
time, though the shade makes it a fair summer morning option too.

Confirm opening hours with the shop before you drive over.
