## Fernandina Beach's local e-bike rules (Ordinance 2026-08)

In July 2026 the Fernandina Beach City Commission unanimously adopted
[Ordinance 2026-08](https://fernandinabeachfl.new.swagit.com/videos/388445), which adds local rules
for bicycles, e-bikes and other personal mobility devices to the city code. As
[reported by the Fernandina Observer](https://www.fernandinaobserver.org/stories/how-fast-can-you-ride-an-e-bike-in-fernandina-beach-new-rules-set-the-limits,112934)
(checked November 11, 2026):

- **25 mph maximum on city roadways**, or the posted limit if lower.
- **10 mph on trail networks, multi-use paths and city-owned beaches**, where pedal assist is allowed.
- **Human power only on sidewalks** and unpaved parts of the public right-of-way, at 10 mph or less.
- **No riding in city recreational parks** unless signs allow it.
- **Penalties:** a citation, with a city safety course or a $50 fine.

These rules cover city limits (the north and central island). The county-maintained Amelia Island
Trail and Amelia River To Sea Trail south of town have their own posted 10 mph limit for all bikes,
with Class 1, 2 and 3 e-bikes allowed, per the
[Friends of the Amelia Island Trail](http://www.ameliaislandtrail.org/about.html).

## The long ride: the Amelia Island Trail

For a proper e-bike day, the [Amelia Island Trail](/trails/amelia-island-trail/) runs a little over
7 shaded, paved miles from Peters Point Beachfront Park to Nassau Sound. Our guide covers the
trailheads, the ARTS Trail detour, parking at Amelia Island State Park and the shops beside the
trail.
