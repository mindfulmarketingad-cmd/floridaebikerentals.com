## Miami Beach's local rules: the Beachwalk is off limits

Miami Beach is one of the few Florida towns where the most famous path in town is closed to
e-bikes. These are the city's rules on top of the statewide ones below, as listed by the City of
Miami Beach on November 3, 2026. Police have run zero-tolerance enforcement on the Beachwalk, so
treat the signs as meant.

**No e-bikes at any time** (or e-scooters, e-skateboards or any other motorized device):

- **The Beachwalk**, the paved path behind the dunes from South Pointe Park up to 79th Street.
- **City sidewalks**, everywhere in Miami Beach, under City Code Section 70-67.
- **South Pointe Park**, including the Cutwalk along Government Cut and the pier.
- **Collins Park.**
- **The Marina Baywalk**, south of 5th Street.
- **The promenade west of the Beachwalk** between 24th and 29th Streets.

**No bikes of any kind**, pedal or electric:

- **The Baywalk** along Biscayne Bay.
- **The Ocean Drive sidewalks** between 5th and 15th Streets.
- **Lincoln Road.** Ordinary bicycles are allowed only from 2 a.m. to 9 a.m.; motorized devices, e-bikes included, are not allowed at any hour.

Mobility devices used by people with disabilities are allowed everywhere. Everywhere else, an
e-bike rides on the street and follows the same traffic signals and laws as a car.

**Reported for July 2027.** News reports say the City Commission approved a safety ordinance in
September 2026 that would fine riders for helmets, lights, brakes and passenger limits, effective
July 22, 2027. The commission item that creates those operator duties is on the
[July 22, 2026 agenda](https://miamibeachagenda.com/agenda/07/22/2026/R5M). We have not found the
adopted section in the published city code, so do not treat a new local fine as in force yet.
[Florida law](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/316.2065) already requires a helmet for
riders and passengers under 16, a white front light and a red rear light and reflector after dark,
working brakes, and no more riders than the bike is designed for.

## Where to ride an e-bike in Miami Beach instead

The city has been adding green-painted bike lanes on both sides of the road. These are the
stretches it lists, and they are where a rental belongs:

- **South Beach:** Ocean Drive between South Pointe Drive and 5th Street, South Pointe Drive between Alton Road and Ocean Drive, Alton Road between 5th Street and South Pointe Drive, and 16th Street between West Avenue and Collins Avenue.
- **Mid-Beach:** Prairie Avenue (West 34th to 44th Streets), Royal Palm Avenue (West 43rd to 47th Streets) and West 47th Street between Alton Road and Pine Tree Drive.
- **North Beach:** Dickens Avenue (71st to 79th Streets) and Byron Avenue (82nd to 89th Streets).

**The Venetian Causeway** is the ride most visitors are really after: bike lanes the whole way
from Purdy Avenue on the Beach to North Bayshore Drive in Miami, over a chain of low bridges and
residential islands, with the downtown skyline ahead and Biscayne Bay on both sides. It carries far
less traffic than the MacArthur, and it is the natural way to ride from the Beach to the city.

**For the ocean view,** ride the streets parallel to the sand and walk the bike onto the Beachwalk
at a beach access point, or lock up at one of the city's 900-plus bike racks and walk it. The city
also has free bike repair stations on the Beachwalk at South Pointe Park, 30th Street and 46th
Street, at Ocean Drive and 14th Street, and on Venetian Way at Island Avenue.

**Lock it every time.** Bike theft is a persistent problem in Miami Beach, rental agreements make
you liable for a stolen bike, and theft is usually excluded from damage waivers (see our
[damage waiver guide](/costs/damage-waivers-and-insurance/)). Six city garages offer free long-term
bike parking, including G5 at 640 17th Street with 65 spaces, if you are stopping for more than an
hour or two.

## FAQs

### Can you ride an e-bike on the Miami Beach Beachwalk?

No. Miami Beach prohibits e-bikes, e-scooters and every other motorized device on the Beachwalk at
all times, from South Pointe Park to 79th Street, and police enforce it. Ride the parallel streets
and bike lanes, and walk the bike if you want to use the Beachwalk.

### Can you ride an e-bike on the sidewalk in Miami Beach?

No. City code bans motorized devices, e-bikes included, on all city sidewalks. Ride on the street,
in the bike lane where there is one, and follow traffic signals as a car would.

### Can you ride an e-bike on Lincoln Road or Ocean Drive?

Not on Lincoln Road: motorized devices are banned there at all hours, and even pedal bikes are
allowed only between 2 a.m. and 9 a.m. Bikes are banned on the Ocean Drive sidewalks from 5th to
15th Streets; the city's bike lane on Ocean Drive runs between South Pointe Drive and 5th Street.

### Where is the best place to ride an e-bike in Miami Beach?

The Venetian Causeway, which has bike lanes from Purdy Avenue across the islands to Miami. On the
Beach itself, use the green bike lanes the city lists in South Beach, Mid-Beach and North Beach.
