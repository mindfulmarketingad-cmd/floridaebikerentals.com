## Jacksonville's local e-bike rules (Ordinance 2026-0570-E)

On September 22, 2026, the Jacksonville City Council unanimously approved
[Ordinance 2026-0570-E](https://jaxcityc.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8144675&GUID=F68F3489-B3BC-4928-9623-EBEDF858D392),
which adds local e-bike rules on top of the state law below. As summarised on the City of
Jacksonville's [e-bike rules page](https://www.jacksonville.gov/departments/planning-department/Transportation-Planning/Ped-Bike-Planning/Bicycle-and-Pedestrian-Advisory-Committee)
(checked November 10, 2026):

- **15 mph maximum on sidewalks and shared-use paths.** You can still be cited below 15 mph for riding unsafely or too fast for conditions.
- **Warn and pass wide.** Give an audible warning (bell or voice) when passing pedestrians or slower cyclists, and pass at a safe distance.
- **Yield to pedestrians** on sidewalks and shared-use paths, and obey pedestrian signs and signals there.
- **Unsafe operation** includes unsafe passing, failing to yield, weaving, stunts, ignoring posted restrictions and leaving the road to get around a stop sign or stoplight.
- **Lights between sunset and sunrise:** a white front light visible from 500 feet, plus a red rear light and red rear reflector visible from 600 feet.
- **Passengers** only on an e-bike with a designated passenger seat.
- **No motor modifications** that let the bike go faster than the manufacturer's designed top speed.
- **Helmets** for riders and passengers under 16, as under state law.

On a rental, the practical upshot is simple: keep it at 15 mph or less on any path or sidewalk, ring
before you pass, and turn the lights on if you will be out after sunset.

## FAQs

### What is the e-bike speed limit in Jacksonville?

15 mph on sidewalks and shared-use paths, under Ordinance 2026-0570-E. On the street, normal traffic
laws and posted limits apply.

### Can you ride an e-bike on the sidewalk in Jacksonville?

The city's rules page treats sidewalks as allowed with conditions: no faster than 15 mph, yield to
pedestrians, give an audible warning when passing and obey pedestrian signals. Watch for posted
restrictions.

### Do you need lights on an e-bike at night in Jacksonville?

Yes. Between sunset and sunrise the city requires a white front light visible from 500 feet and a
red rear light and red rear reflector visible from 600 feet.
